# ECO00 Global Economic Capability Protocol

ECO00 is a deterministic, repo-side opportunity normalization and policy layer. A registered capability or available API is never permission. New capability families default to dry-run and may be added as data without changing the core engine.

Money and risk use bounded integer cents and basis points. Ranking considers risk-adjusted profit, expected profit, margin, capital, time, and stable identity; raw revenue never ranks alone. The SQLite candidate store preserves opportunity identity and a hash-chained event history across restart.

Owner-supplied business capital defaults to zero cents. Purchase, acquisition, deposit, fulfillment, inventory, and other capital components must reconcile exactly to declared capital. Any positive requirement blocks execution readiness without explicit Owner authorization. Infrastructure and model spend remain separate under CP11/CP13.

Lifecycle transitions fail closed. External execution requires Owner authorization. Only verified Acceptance Gate evidence can move an opportunity from `ACCEPTANCE_PENDING` to `ACCEPTED`; model, agent, platform, marketplace, or self-report cannot do so.

Platform entries are data-only and default to `DRY_RUN`. ECO00 includes no live marketplace writer, customer outreach, listing publication, purchase, or paid call. eBay retail/marketplace direct fulfillment is prohibited; Upwork remains `ACCESS_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and no account/API access is assumed.
