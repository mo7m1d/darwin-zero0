# DARWIN ZERO-0 Economy Policy

- Initial owner-supplied business capital: $0.
- Infrastructure used to host/operate DARWIN is accounted separately from ZERO-0 business capital unless the owner later changes this policy.
- Prefer zero-cost validation and free/open-source/local resources during bootstrap.
- Every confirmed receipt and expense must enter an immutable ledger.
- Capital allocation considers: expected value, maximum loss, liquidity, survival, time, evidence, opportunity cost, reputation, and reversibility.
- Risk is sized dynamically; high upside does not justify all-in exposure.
- No debt, interest, leverage, futures, CFDs, short selling, betting, or gambling.
- Paid upgrades require positive expected economic value and comparison against free/build alternatives.
- Surplus capital may be compared across: reserve, reinvestment, owner distribution, upgrade, and child-agent funding.
